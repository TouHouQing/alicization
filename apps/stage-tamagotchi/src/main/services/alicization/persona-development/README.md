# Personality development

## Authority

The active SOUL and its managed learned-personality section hold adopted traits.
`life:persona-development` owns hypotheses, bounded trials and source references.
It cannot change identity, manual directives, execution permissions or risk policy.
Autobiographical maintenance does not constitute another learning event.

## Experience to behavior

1. Read confirmed reflections, reinforcement records and independently verified,
   actually proposed personal projects through the local SQLite reader.
2. Resolve card, version, source availability, sensitive-data and contamination
   checks before sending cleaned evidence to the configured model.
3. Extract at most two concrete, scoped hypotheses per background run. Preserve
   existing hypothesis identities and record independent originating turns.
4. Begin one small trial per actual dialogue turn after three independent events
   spanning two days. A trial has an exact request hash, context and owned turn.
5. Settle a trial only from structured feedback about that exact reply. Generic
   reinforcement is not success, negative feedback is not positive merely because
   it reinforces an improvement direction, and interruption is unknown.
6. A mature hypothesis requires eight independent events, seven represented days,
   six days since the first witness and three successful trials. These are product
   guardrails, not a scientific claim about consciousness or human personality.
7. Prepare an exact SOUL diff, preserve the stable core, recheck evidence during
   activation and bind the adopted version to the active SOUL hash.

Dialogue consumes typed projections. Mature projections also influence the
existing initiative ranking and shared Live2D performance path. Emotion, motion
selection, phonemes, verification requirements and execution risk retain their
existing owners. No new gesture loop or unsolicited timer is introduced.

## Corrections and forgetting

Pausing suspends new learning and trials while preserving mature traits.
A correction adds a concrete exception and starts a new hypothesis version.
Old experiences and trial successes cannot automatically promote that revision.
Disputed hypotheses can explicitly return to observation. Withdrawal reserves
the identity in a local tombstone even after its display record is pruned.

Source invalidation follows the existing evidence dependency graph. Explicit
forgetting withdraws dependent hypotheses, clears their readable derived text and
prevents replay. SOUL replacement is fenced against concurrent manual edits and
source withdrawals. Development staging copies are retired after activation or
failure; interrupted replacements retain the existing recovery protocol.

## Budgets and storage

- At most 64 displayed hypotheses and 32 adopted facets.
- At most four mature facets and one trial in a dialogue context.
- Trial effects limited to ±0.03; mature effects to ±0.15; aggregate to ±0.2.
- Full independent-root ledger retained for deduplication and audit.
- Review decisions sample up to eight witnesses from each of 16 represented days.
  A busy day cannot evict all earlier days. Sources are rechecked before use.
- Sensitive or contaminated sources are acknowledged locally without transmitting
  their text, so rejected history cannot permanently block later eligible records.
- Background extraction stops for foreground work or cancellation. Committed
  source acknowledgements survive restart independently of in-memory scheduling.

## Optional model training

Local behavioral adaptation does not require weight-training consent. Weight
training retains its separate cleaning, consent, PII and dataset policy. Core
persona revisions retain exact paired evaluation and explicit human review.
Verified work procedures remain procedural knowledge; completing a task does not
prove a personality trait or mastery of a general skill.

## Validation

`repository.test.ts` exercises actual SQLite ownership, replay/restart, scoped
trials, feedback attribution, correction, SOUL adoption, withdrawal and races.
`scale.test.ts` seeds 100,000 independent reviewed synthetic witnesses and checks
the bounded cross-day review. Configured-model cases run with
`LIFE_QA_CONFIGURED_GROWTH=1`; all inputs are synthetic QA experiences.

The product fixture and Playwright checks under `scripts/life-acceptance` exercise
the actual settings controls, model turn and Live2D drawing in a background
viewport. Synthetic historical dates do not establish elapsed longitudinal use.
