NSIS per-user installation directory fix adapted from electron-builder:
https://github.com/electron-userland/electron-builder/commit/a356198ec7c54c7795659342bff36d9a5162cd93
Source: packages/app-builder-lib/templates/nsis/multiUser.nsh
Local adaptation: build/nsis-safe-per-user.nsh. The 26.8.1 multiUser.nsh
template is vendored in build/nsis/multiUser.nsh; its only change routes the
per-user macro to this fix before macro expansion. The standard builder
installer and signed-uninstaller generation are retained. Nested runtime hook
replacement is avoided for compatibility with the pinned NSIS compiler.
