## Migration decisions

Existing autobiographical snapshots acquire an optional evidence fingerprint on their next projection. The fingerprint prevents maintenance-only rebuilds from repeatedly moving learned preferences. Current goals and emotional/context projections remain live. Historical snapshots are preserved; no raw transcript is admitted as training data.

The checkpoint also retains up to 512 recently observed originating roots. Changes to row IDs, derived wording or recent-window membership do not constitute another learning event. Stable behavior signatures follow that same clock. The full personality evidence ledger remains durable and is not limited to that legacy projection cache.

Life schema 8 adds the personality evidence, exact-turn trials, extraction acknowledgements and withdrawal tombstones. The released v7 store receives a consistent SQLite backup before migration. Existing relationship/personhood state is preserved; the recovery backup retains the original schema version.

Core changes retain exact-candidate review. Low-risk local adaptation will use independently validated local evidence and must not depend on training consent. Training permissions remain independently enforced.
